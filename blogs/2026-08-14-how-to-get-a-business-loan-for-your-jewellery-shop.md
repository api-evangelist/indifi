Key takeaways: Running a jewellery shop means your money sits on display shelves. When wedding season approaches or gold prices dip, you need capital fast. But traditional lenders often ask you to pledge the very inventory you need to sell, which is why most jewellery loan products are moving toward cash-flow-based, collateral-free lending.
