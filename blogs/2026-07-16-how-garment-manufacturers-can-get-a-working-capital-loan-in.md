Key Takeaways Garment manufacturers in India can obtain collateral-free working capital loans of up to ₹50 lakh from digital lenders and NBFCs, typically disbursed within 48 hours. Basic eligibility includes 1+ years of business vintage, ₹12 lakh+ in annual turnover, and regular GST filings. No property pledge required.
